# Shepherd's Path Product Conviction Charter

## Why We Exist

Shepherd's Path exists to help people draw near to God through Scripture, prayer, worship, reflection, and faithful daily discipleship.

We build as stewards, not owners.  
We serve Christ's name, not our own.

## Foundational Convictions

1. **God is enough.**  
   We trust God to transform hearts. Product design supports surrender; it does not manufacture spiritual fruit.

2. **Jesus is the center.**  
   The app must point users toward Christ, not toward platform attachment, personality cults, or spiritual performance.

3. **Scripture is authority.**  
   God's Word is primary. AI and product systems are supporting instruments, never replacements for Scripture.

4. **Technology is a servant.**  
   AI is used to guide, clarify, and encourage biblical engagement. It is not treated as a spiritual authority.

5. **Stewardship over self-exaltation.**  
   We reject ego-driven decisions. We pursue excellence so Christ is honored and people are served faithfully.

6. **Formation over stimulation.**  
   We optimize for prayerfulness, peace, repentance, trust, and consistency—not dopamine loops, vanity metrics, or addictive behavior.

7. **Community over comparison.**  
   We foster encouragement, intercession, and shared faithfulness. We reject social mechanics that promote pride, shame, or competition.

## Product Commitments

- **Christ-first language:** Copy and UX should invite humility, surrender, hope, and obedience.
- **Scripture-first experience:** Core paths should increase real engagement with God's Word.
- **Prayer-first rhythms:** Features should guide users into honest and consistent prayer.
- **Peace-first design:** Calm, uncluttered, reverent interfaces that reduce noise and distraction.
- **Integrity-first monetization:** Core discipleship and spiritual essentials remain accessible; paid depth never exploits spiritual hunger.
- **Care-first safety:** Sensitive moments are handled with pastoral seriousness and emotionally safe guidance.

## What We Explicitly Reject

- Shallow gamification
- Vanity metrics as spiritual proxies
- Addictive engagement loops
- Performative spirituality
- Shame-based retention
- Social comparison as motivation
- Product decisions that make AI appear spiritually authoritative

## Decision Filter (Use Before Shipping)

Every meaningful feature, campaign, or copy change must pass these questions:

1. **Does this exalt Christ or elevate us?**
2. **Does this keep Scripture central and clear?**
3. **Does this help users pray, obey, and grow in faithfulness?**
4. **Does this protect humility, peace, and authenticity?**
5. **Would we still ship this if it lowered engagement but improved spiritual integrity?**

If any answer is "no," we redesign or do not ship.

## Team Prayer Posture

Lord, keep us faithful in little and much.  
Guard us from pride, urgency without wisdom, and growth without fruit.  
Let this work serve Your people, honor Your Word, and glorify Your name.  
Amen.
