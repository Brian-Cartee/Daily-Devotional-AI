# Small group desirability test (4–6 people)

**Purpose:** Confirm people *want* this — not just that it works — before VC, church, or partner conversations.

**Not a usability lab.** You’re listening for: Did they feel less alone? Would they come back? Did anything feel off spiritually or emotionally?

---

## Who to invite (4–6)

Mix matters more than size:

| Person | Why |
|--------|-----|
| 1 | In a heavy season now (anxiety, grief, loneliness, burnout) |
| 1 | Faithful but not “church every Sunday” |
| 1 | Skeptical of AI or apps — honest critic |
| 1 | Less comfortable with tech — types slowly, maybe older |
| 1–2 | Warm supporters who’ll still tell you the truth |

**Avoid:** Only people who will praise you. One gentle critic is worth three cheerleaders.

---

## What you ask them to do (pick one path each)

Send this verbatim (text or email):

> I’m testing something I built called Shepherd’s Path — a quiet place for Scripture and prayer when life is heavy. Would you try **one** of these in the next 3–5 days, alone, when you actually have something on your heart — not as a favor to me, but as a real moment?
>
> **Option A:** Open the app → **Talk It Through** → share something honestly (type or speak) → read through what you get.  
> **Option B:** **Today** (daily devotional) → one full pass: verse, reflection, prayer.
>
> Afterward, I’ll ask you 5 short questions. No wrong answers. Takes ~15 min total.

Link: https://www.shepherdspathai.com (or App Store if they’re on iPhone)

---

## Five questions (voice memo, text, or 10-min call)

1. **What were you carrying when you opened it?** (context — was it a real moment?)
2. **What did you feel while using it?** (seen, rushed, comforted, weird, robotic, peaceful?)
3. **Talk It Through / Today:** Would you use this again **without me asking**? Why or why not?
4. **One thing that worked** — be specific.
5. **One thing that would stop you from coming back** — be honest.

**Bonus if they’ll share:** “Would you tell someone who was hurting to try this?”

---

## Desirability bar (go / no-go for partner outreach)

**Green light (proceed to brother / church / VC conversations):**

- At least **3 of 4–6** say they’d use it again unprompted
- At least **1** unprompted emotional quote you’d be proud to repeat (“I felt less alone,” “I actually prayed,” “I’d use this at 2 AM”)
- **Zero** serious spiritual red flags (felt preached at, unsafe, “AI pretending to be God,” creepy)

**Yellow (another bug-fix + retest round):**

- Mixed “nice but wouldn’t return”
- Confusion on what Talk It Through is for
- Technical friction blocked the moment (voice, loading, paywall surprise)

**Red (fix before any external pitch):**

- Multiple testers felt manipulated, cold, or theologically off
- Core flow broken on device
- Free tier felt like a bait-and-switch

---

## What to capture (simple)

Create a note per person:

```
Name / relationship:
Path used (TTT or Today):
Would return? Y / N / Maybe:
Best quote:
Blocker:
```

After all sessions, write **3 sentences** for yourself:

1. What surprised you  
2. What you’ll fix before the next test  
3. Whether you’re ready to talk to your brother  

---

## Group use vs. church pilot (later)

This test is **individual**, Shepherd's Path brand, no white label. Church/group structure comes **after** you know individuals want it. Your brother conversation on white label vs. branded group use stays on hold until you have desirability proof.
