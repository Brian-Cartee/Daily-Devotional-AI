# Mission-Fit Backlog

Purpose: prioritize product work that deepens discipleship while protecting sacred tone, humility, and ministry integrity.

Reference: `docs/PRODUCT_CONVICTION_CHARTER.md`

## 1) Ship Now (High Mission Fit, Low Risk)

1. **Sacred onboarding completion polish**
   - Season + covenant capture, immediate first-prayer handoff, gentle completion language.
2. **Take-a-moment readability**
   - Mobile-safe verse excerpt with full scripture on expand.
3. **Three starter journeys**
   - `7 Days of Prayer`, `Psalms for Anxiety`, `Worship Before Work`.
4. **Private faithfulness visualization**
   - Prayer/scripture/stillness/service view, private by default, no scores.
5. **Calm notification controls**
   - Max daily cap, quiet windows, no urgency phrasing.
6. **Weekly peace reflection**
   - Short check-in centered on peace, trust, and scripture response.

## 2) Test Carefully (Potentially Strong, Needs Guardrails)

1. **Trusted accountability circles**
   - Small private groups with prayer prompts; must avoid social pressure.
2. **Testimony sharing**
   - Opt-in only; avoid engagement-seeking framing and visible popularity signals.
3. **Journey reminders**
   - Helpful cadence without guilt messaging.
4. **Premium depth experiences**
   - Audio liturgies, guided reflections, seasonal tracks; keep essentials free.
5. **Emotion-state prompts**
   - Useful for care pathways; avoid labeling users as identity categories.

## 3) Avoid (Mission Misalignment / High Spiritual Risk)

1. Public streak leaderboards
2. Rank, badge, or level systems tied to spiritual worth
3. Competitive faith challenges
4. Reaction-count feeds for prayers/testimonies
5. Fear/FOMO push copy ("don't break your streak", "last chance")
6. Paywalling core prayer/scripture access
7. Any AI framing that implies authority over Scripture

## Practical Release Rule

For every planned feature, label it:
- `ship-now`
- `test-carefully`
- `avoid`

If a `test-carefully` feature cannot pass charter gates in design review, it is automatically moved to `avoid`.
