## Filming checklist (each Short)

- [ ] Hook in **first 2 seconds** (text on screen + spoken)
- [ ] One verse or one honest line (not a sermon)
- [ ] **No** prosperity language, no guilt
- [ ] End card **3 sec** (template above)
- [ ] Description pasted **before** publish
- [ ] Pinned comment with link (long-form only; Shorts = description link)
- [ ] `#shorts` + 2 tags max (e.g. `#dailydevotional` `#faith`)

## Five ready-to-film scripts (25–35 sec)

Tone: cinematic, quiet, premium. Speak like a friend, not a preacher.

### 1 — “When God feels far” (awareness)

| Sec | Visual | Audio / text on screen |
|-----|--------|-------------------------|
| 0–3 | Face or window light | **TEXT:** "When did God start feeling far?" |
| 3–15 | Slow b-roll (coffee, Bible, phone face-down) | "It isn't always doubt. Sometimes you're just tired. Scripture still meets you in that place." |
| 15–25 | Verse on screen (your choice) | Read one line — e.g. Psalm 46:10 fragment |
| 25–35 | End card | "Free daily devotional — link in description" |

**Title:** When God Feels Far (You're Not Broken)

---

### 2 — “Daily devotional in 3 steps” (product)

| Sec | Visual | Audio / text |
|-----|--------|----------------|
| 0–3 | Screen record or 3 icons | **TEXT:** "3 steps. No overwhelm." |
| 3–12 | Verse card | "Today's verse — sit with it." |
| 12–22 | Reflection blur (no private text) | "A short reflection. Then a prayer you can pray as your own." |
| 22–35 | End card | "Shepherd's Path — free link below" |

**Title:** A Daily Devotional That Doesn't Overwhelm You

**Note:** If you screen-record, show **Today** tab → devotional — not settings.

---

### 3 — “Your name in the prayer” (personalization)

| Sec | Visual | Audio / text |
|-----|--------|----------------|
| 0–3 | Phone in hand | **TEXT:** "Generic devotionals miss you." |
| 3–18 | Tap Reflection (blur if needed) | "Add your first name once. Today's reflection and prayer can include *you* — gently, not cheesy." |
| 18–35 | End card | "Try it free — Shepherd's Path" |

**Title:** A Devotional That Knows Your Name

---

### 4 — “3am anxiety” (night)

| Sec | Visual | Audio / text |
|-----|--------|----------------|
| 0–3 | Dark room, soft light | **TEXT:** "Awake at 3am again?" |
| 3–20 | Still b-roll | "You don't need a lecture. You need one true verse and room to breathe. That's enough for tonight." |
| 20–35 | End card | "Quiet devotional — link below" |

**Title:** For the 3am Scroll (Try This Instead)

---

### 5 — “Not another church app” (positioning)

| Sec | Visual | Audio / text |
|-----|--------|----------------|
| 0–3 | Calm face or path outdoors | **TEXT:** "This isn't church on your phone." |
| 3–22 | App ambience shots | "It's a daily companion — verse, reflection, prayer, journal. For when life is loud and you want God without the noise." |
| 22–35 | End card | "Free — shepherdspathai.com" |

**Title:** Not Church on Your Phone (A Quiet Daily Companion)

## Thumbnail / title formulas

- **Emotion + specificity:** "When God Feels Silent at 2am"
- **Not clickbait:** avoid "God will FIX your life in 24 hours"
- **3–6 words** on thumbnail; full title in YouTube
