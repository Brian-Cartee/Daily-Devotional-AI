## PART 1 — POSITIONING

### Niche to dominate

**Quiet faith for overwhelmed adults** — not debate, not worship hype, not church drama.

- **YouTube:** Faith & spirituality → Christian living for anxious / burned-out adults  
- **Emotional:** Peace + meaning (not therapy replacement)  
- **Behavioral:** Daily rhythm (2–7 minutes)  
- **Product:** Private companion app (not social network)

### Emotional transformation

> *"I don't have to perform my faith. I can return to God quietly, honestly, and daily — without shame."*

1. Relief → 2. Presence → 3. Rhythm → 4. Depth

### Different from typical Christian content

| Typical | Shepherd's Path |
|---------|-----------------|
| Loud, culture war | Cinematic, invitational |
| Performative holiness | Permission to be tired, returning |
| Pastor celebrity | Gentle companion |
| Church as destination | Private closet / chapel |

**Brand line:** *Shepherd's Path isn't church on your phone. It's a quiet room for prayer, Scripture, and daily guidance.*

| Segment | Time | Job |
|---------|------|-----|
| Cold open | 0:00–0:20 | Emotional mirror |

| Soft product | 9:30–10:30 | App as tool for practice |
| Close | 10:30+ | Blessing, no hype |

### Length by platform

| Platform | Length |
|----------|--------|
| YouTube Shorts | 22–38s |
| Reels / TikTok | 18–34s |
| Long-form | 9–14 min |

### CTA ladder (non-salesy)

1. Comment: "Type 'still' if you needed this."  
2. Playlist: Rebuild series  
3. Link: Free daily devotional (install)  
4. Pro: Only after trust — "go deeper when ready"

### Free → paid (integrity)

- Pro after 3+ meaningful free sessions  
- Upgrade at real limits (deep guidance, journal depth)  
- Never tie salvation to subscription

## PART 5 — YOUTUBE ALGORITHM

- Shorts → verbal bridge to long-form + pinned comment  
- Long-form → chapters + description link + pinned "today's verse in app"  
- **Cadence:** 5–7 Shorts/week, 1 long-form/week, 2 lives/month  
- Thumbnails: 3–5 words, purple-dark, one emotion word  
- Comments: pin "What are you carrying tonight?" — reply with short prayers

### KPI targets

### Phase 1 (Days 1–30)

Channel setup, 30 Shorts bank, series *60 Seconds of Stillness*, first long-form, app screen record. **CTA: free only.**

### Phase 2 (Days 31–60)

*Men & Quiet Cross*, micro-creator collab, complete *Rebuild 7-day*, UTM links in descriptions.

### Phase 3 (Days 61–90)

Authentic app demo video, Sunday Night Reset live, gentle Pro mentions, referral push.

### Weekly checklist

- [ ] 6 Shorts scheduled  
- [ ] 1 long-form filmed  
- [ ] 1 carousel + 1 email  
- [ ] 20 min comment replies  
- [ ] Review UTM / install data  
- [ ] Match in-app theme to top video

## Guardrails

No prosperity gospel, fear-based Pro, fake testimonies, or shame engagement bait.

**Strategy in one line:** Shorts heal the scroll; long-form builds trust; the app hosts the daily return; Pro deepens — never saves.
