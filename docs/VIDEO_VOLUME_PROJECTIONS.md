## Video formats & durations (use these consistently)

| Format | Length | Primary job | Production time (solo, realistic) |
|--------|--------|-------------|-------------------------------------|
| **YouTube Short** | **22–38 sec** (target **30 sec**) | Discovery, hooks, emotion | **45–90 min** each (script → film → CapCut → publish) |
| **Reel / TikTok** (repurpose) | **18–34 sec** | Same as Short; trim + re-export | **+15 min** per Short if same day |
| **Long-form** | **9–14 min** (target **10 min**) | Trust, depth, playlist SEO | **5–8 hours** each (outline → film → edit → thumb → desc) |
| **Live** (optional) | **20–40 min** | Community, comments | **2–3 hours** incl. prep |
| **Carousel / static** (repurpose) | 5 slides | IG/FB; from long script | **30–45 min** from existing script |

**Repurpose rule (from master plan):** 1 long-form → **3 Shorts** + carousel + email snippet. That’s **4 publishable assets** from one ~6-hour block.

## Funnel math (views → installs)

Rough industry ranges for **quiet faith / app CTA** content (your niche, not hype):

| Step | Conservative | Moderate | Strong |
|------|--------------|----------|--------|
| Short view → tap link | 0.2% | 0.5% | 1.2% |
| Tap → open/install site | 25% | 35% | 45% |
| **Effective: views per install** | **~2,000** | **~570** | **~185** |

**Long-form** often converts **3–8× better** per view than Shorts (more trust, longer CTA window).

### Installs needed → Shorts views (Shorts-only traffic)

| Installs goal | Conservative views | Moderate views | Strong views |
|---------------|-------------------|----------------|--------------|
| **4,000** (90-day plan) | **8M** | **2.3M** | **740k** |
| **15,000** (wild Y1) | **30M** | **8.5M** | **2.8M** |
| **50,000** (moonshot) | **100M** | **28M** | **9M** |

Your master plan’s **3M Shorts views / 30d by day 90** + long-form + word of mouth ≈ **4k installs** is **moderate-to-strong** conversion — plausible if CTAs and app store path are clean.

## When projections break

- **No UTM / weak CTA** → installs lag despite views  
- **Broken app path** (name lost on iOS) → installs don’t stick → MAU & paid lag (**fix with iOS build**)  
- **Inconsistent posting** → views don’t compound  
- **Generic hooks** → need 10× the views in funnel table  

Review **installs per week** in analytics monthly; adjust tier up only if conversion justifies more hours.
