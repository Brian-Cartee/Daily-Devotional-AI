# Philip Device Test Script — Tomorrow

**Build:** Philip Runtime 0.2.7 (post Fix A + Fix C)  
**Where:** Talk It Through on your phone (Shepherd's Path app)  
**Time:** ~20–30 minutes total  
**Goal:** Does Philip feel like a person listening — not a script rotating comfort lines?

---

## Before you start

1. Confirm live build is recent:
   ```bash
   curl -s https://www.shepherdspathai.com/native-manifest.json
   ```
2. Open **Talk It Through** (not Voice Lab unless you want a second pass later).
3. Have this doc on another screen — you don't need to read Philip's lines aloud verbatim.

---

## What "good" looks like

- Philip **mirrors your actual words** before asking anything
- He **doesn't repeat** the same short line twice ("I hear you," "I'm not going anywhere")
- He **doesn't** say things like "How does grief show up in this for you right now?"
- He **doesn't** apologize for being a script unless you called that out
- On grief threads, by exchange **8+** he should offer a **send-off** — permission to stop, no question

## What "bad" looks like (stop and note it)

- Same comfort phrase twice in one conversation
- Clinical worksheet questions
- "I kept handing you lines instead of hearing you" when you didn't accuse him
- Ignoring a direct question (privacy, "is this selfish?", "what do you think?")

---

## Test 1 — Grief + send-off (~8 min)

**Open with:**
> My dad died two months ago and I still cry every day. His coffee mug is still on the counter and I can't move it.

**Then follow naturally.** Add detail if he asks — mornings, phone not ringing, etc.

**Watch for:**
- Exchange 2–4: Does he receive your images (mug, phone) or jump to generic probes?
- Exchange 6–8: If you push back ("you keep saying things like that"), does he recover with substance — not another stock line?
- Exchange 8+: Does he send you off — permission to stop, **no question**?

**Pass if:** You feel heard on the mug/dad specifics; no stock-line loop; send-off feels natural by end.

---

## Test 2 — Almost-said-it (~8 min)

**Open with:**
> There's something I've been wanting to say but I don't know if this makes sense.

**Go slow.** Don't rush the disclosure. Hover at the edge.

**Watch for:**
- Early turns: Space, not probes ("What happened next?", "How does fear show up…")
- Mid turns: Mirrors your words, not rotating gratitude lines
- If you name the loop ("you keep saying that"), he should acknowledge and receive — not meta-apologize

**Pass if:** You feel room to approach something hard; he doesn't interrogate before you've offered anything.

---

## Test 3 — Confession / shame (~8 min)

**Open with:**
> Honestly I've never said this out loud — I don't think God could forgive me for what I did.

**Share slowly.** Don't explain the act immediately.

**Watch for:**
- Sacred pause: one-sentence receive, no question, no early scripture
- No virtue-labeling ("that took courage") when you're naming pain
- If you ask him something directly, he should answer — not dodge with a send-off line

**Pass if:** Shame thread feels held, not managed or rushed toward reassurance.

---

## Optional — Anger at church (~5 min)

**Open with:**
> I went to church last Sunday and the whole thing felt like a performance. I sat there and felt nothing.

**Watch for:** No four stock lines in a row. He should engage the performance wound, not rotate "I hear you."

## After each test — 3 quick notes

Use `docs/philip-review/templates/SESSION_REVIEW.md` or jot:

2. **Worst moment** — where the illusion broke (quote him if you can)
3. **Would you come back?** Yes / Maybe / No — one sentence why
